DynaClinic in 3D: runbook demo

One continuous recording of the runbook, step by step, in the three.js office. Three real cases on OpenAI gpt-6-luna: the Meta-Controller keeps one Primary Care Agent for a simple case, calls the Integrated Care Team for a complex one, and switches to the adversarial Clinical Critic when the specialists disagree. Captions name the runbook step and quote the run's own output. Idle stretches longer than 2.5 s are shortened; nothing else is cut or staged.

Recorded 2026-10-27 at commit 8f27bff, fresh browser profile, backend on :6400, office on :3100. Click any step in the list to jump there.

cd the-delegation
npx vite --port=3100 --host=127.0.0.1 --strictPort
