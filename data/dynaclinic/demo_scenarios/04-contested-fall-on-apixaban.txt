Mock case: 74-year-old woman on apixaban, short of breath after a fall

A 74-year-old woman with atrial fibrillation on apixaban 5 mg twice daily, hypertension, and chronic kidney disease (eGFR 38 mL/min/1.73m2) fell at home 6 hours ago and now has new dyspnea and pleuritic chest pain. She is alert, GCS 15, without focal deficits. Heart rate 112/min, blood pressure 104/66 mm Hg, SpO2 90% on room air. Non-contrast CT head shows a 6-mm acute left frontal subdural hematoma without midline shift. CT pulmonary angiography shows bilateral segmental pulmonary emboli with an RV/LV ratio of 1.1. Troponin is mildly elevated. Hemoglobin 11.2 g/dL, platelets 190,000/mm3.
