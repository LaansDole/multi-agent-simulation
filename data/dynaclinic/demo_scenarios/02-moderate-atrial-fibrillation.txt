Mock case: 72-year-old woman with newly found atrial fibrillation

A 72-year-old woman is found to have atrial fibrillation at a routine visit. She has hypertension and type 2 diabetes. Medications are lisinopril, metformin, and metoprolol, which was started last week and has controlled her heart rate to 78/min. Blood pressure is 132/80 mm Hg. ECG shows an irregularly irregular rhythm without P waves. Transthoracic echocardiography shows normal valves and a normal ejection fraction. eGFR is 70 mL/min/1.73 m2 and hemoglobin is 13.2 g/dL. She has no history of bleeding and no allergies.

Age: 72
Sex: female
History: atrial fibrillation; hypertension; type 2 diabetes
Medications: lisinopril 20 mg daily; metformin 1 g twice daily; metoprolol succinate 50 mg daily
Allergies: none
Labs: eGFR 70 mL/min/1.73 m2; hemoglobin 13.2 g/dL
Imaging: ECG: irregularly irregular rhythm without P waves, ventricular rate 78/min; Transthoracic echocardiogram: normal valves, ejection fraction 60%
