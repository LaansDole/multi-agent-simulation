Mock case: Pregnant woman at 30 weeks with chest pain and shortness of breath

A 29-year-old woman at 30 weeks of gestation presents with 1 day of pleuritic chest pain and dyspnea. She has mild swelling of the left calf. Heart rate 108/min, blood pressure 118/72 mm Hg, SpO2 95% on room air. Chest radiograph is normal. D-dimer is elevated. She takes prenatal vitamins only. Creatinine is 0.6 mg/dL.

Age: 29
Sex: female
History: pregnancy, 30 weeks of gestation
Medications: prenatal vitamins
Allergies: none
Labs: D-dimer 1.9 ug/mL FEU; creatinine 0.6 mg/dL
Imaging: Chest radiograph: normal

Question: Which of the following is the most appropriate next step in management?
A. CT pulmonary angiography
B. Ventilation-perfusion scan
C. Start low-molecular-weight heparin and obtain compression ultrasonography of the left leg
D. Start warfarin
E. Reassure and discharge because D-dimer is physiologically elevated in pregnancy
