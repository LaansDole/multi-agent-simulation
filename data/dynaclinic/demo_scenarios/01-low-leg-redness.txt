Mock case: 34-year-old man with a warm, red lower leg

A 34-year-old man presents with 2 days of a spreading, warm, tender, erythematous area on his left lower leg after a minor scrape while gardening. He has no medical history and takes no medications. He developed a rash after taking a sulfonamide antibiotic in the past. White blood cell count is 9,800/mm3.
